Don't wait on these

Five signs your cooling system needs us

A small leak today is a cracked head or a blown engine next month. Catch it early and it usually costs a lot less.

Call 905-738-5235
  • Temperature gauge climbingRunning hotter than normal, especially in traffic or with the A/C on.
  • Green, orange or pink puddlesCoolant under the front of the car means a leak somewhere in the system.
  • Sweet smell or steamFrom under the hood or through the vents. Often a radiator or heater core leak.
  • A/C blowing warmLow refrigerant, a failing condenser or a compressor problem.
  • Heater won't get hotLow coolant, a stuck thermostat or a plugged heater core.
